If the alert is a volatility spike, verify whether realized volatility is still expanding on the next 1 to 3 bars before escalating.
If drawdown deepens while correlation jumps, treat the event as market-linked stress rather than isolated noise.
If the signal is a trend break, check whether prior support is reclaimed quickly; failed reclaim is more important than the first break itself.
If the event is liquidity stress, note that execution quality and slippage may deteriorate faster than simple VaR estimates imply.
When memoing to stakeholders, separate what happened, why risk increased, and what must be monitored next.
